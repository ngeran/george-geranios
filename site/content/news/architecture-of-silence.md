title: "The Architecture of Silence"
date: 2023-09-28
summary: "Removing the human element to reveal the skeletal truth of our environments."
---

Removing the human element from the frame to reveal the skeletal truth of our environments. A study on void space and atmospheric neutrality.

The gallery void becomes its own subject: a space so emptied of incident that the architecture itself must carry the entire weight of meaning. Silence, here, is not absence but structure.
