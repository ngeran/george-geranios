Removing the human element from the frame to reveal the atmospheric neutrality of the landscape. A study on void space and the clarity that distance provides.

Captured at the solar fields of the Atacama — thousands of panels arranged in perfect concentric geometry, reflecting a pale and indifferent sky. The composition is centred and symmetrical; the intersection of technology and landscape is total.
