An exploration into the rigid geometry of urban planning. How the intersection of verticality and repetition creates a rhythm that demands objective distance from the viewer.

The series examines the skeletal truth of modern environments — vast logistical architectures whose scale only resolves when the human element is removed from the frame. What appears at first as abstraction reveals, on sustained looking, an implacable order.
