A comprehensive catalogue raisonné chronicling four decades of work. The volume documents all primary photographs produced between 1980 and 2020, arranged in systematic chronological order, with full provenance, exhibition history, and bibliographic references for each entry.

Essays by leading curators situate the practice within the broader history of large-format photography and the documentary tradition.
